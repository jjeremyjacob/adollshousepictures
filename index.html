ABOUT

A Doll’s House Pictures was founded by Jeremy Jacob, a director and creative working across film, photography, animation, and design.

For nearly two decades, Jeremy has worked in production design, art direction, and directing, collaborating with artists, cultural institutions, and brands to create distinctive visual work that is thoughtful, precise, and grounded in its subject.

A Doll’s House Pictures brings these disciplines together, developing images, films, and visual worlds from the first idea to the final edit.
