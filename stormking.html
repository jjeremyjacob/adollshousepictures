01 — Girl Group

Performance / Highlights
Performance documentation

Girl Group was a performance collaboration between artist Arlene Shechet and choreographer Annie-B Parson, presented at Storm King Art Center in July and September 2024. Developed in response to Shechet's six monumental outdoor sculptures, the work combined choreography and sculpture in a sequence of site-specific performances across the Art Center's landscape.

02 — Growth: The Watch

Performance / Highlights
Kevin Beasley

Growth: The Watch is a collaborative performance by artist Kevin Beasley, developed in conjunction with his site-specific installation PROSCENIUM | Rebirth / Growth: The Watch / Harvest / Dormancy: On Reflection (2024–2025) at Storm King Art Center. The performance activates the installation through sound, movement, and live participation, extending Beasley's exploration of landscape, material history, and the relationship between people and place. The larger installation comprises four seasonal triptychs constructed from cast-resin panels incorporating clothing, plants, agricultural materials, and other collected elements. Growth: The Watch represents the summer component of the work.
